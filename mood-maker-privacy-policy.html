Privacy Policy

Mood Maker

Effective Date: July 30, 2026

Mood Maker is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the application handles information.

Information We Collect

The current version of Mood Maker does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information.

The app does not require:

  • User accounts
  • Login or registration
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Location

Information Stored on Your Device

Mood Maker stores the following information only on your own device:

  • Favorite quotes
  • App preferences
  • Reminder settings

This information never leaves your device.

Notifications

If you enable daily reminders, the application schedules notifications locally on your device. No notification data is collected or shared.

Third-Party Services

The current version of Mood Maker does not use:

  • Advertising services
  • Analytics services
  • Cloud storage
  • User tracking technologies

Children's Privacy

Mood Maker does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated in future versions. Any changes will appear on this page with an updated effective date.
